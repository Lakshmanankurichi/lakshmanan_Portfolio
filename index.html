      06AboutChennai, IN

      I'm an Electronics Engineering graduate who took a long detour into design and never went back. Seven years later I work on the kind of products people don't choose — enterprise platforms they have to use every day — which is exactly why the craft matters.

      My approach is research-led: validate the requirement, map the workflow, make the decisions explicit, then design. I'm currently pursuing an MBA to sharpen the business side of that conversation.
